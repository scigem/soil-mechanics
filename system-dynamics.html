System Dynamics

A system dynamics model follows a stock x through time by its rate of change dx/dt, the balance of what flows in and out, which depends on x itself. This page solves a family of first-order models: exponential growth and decay; a constant input u with decay, which settles at u/r; and logistic growth towards a carrying capacity k, with and without a harvest. With a constant harvest h, the normalised harvest H = 4h/(rk) compares it with the largest growth the logistic model can supply, rk/4 at x = k/2. Below 1 there are two equilibria; at 1 they merge at k/2; above 1 there are none and the stock collapses.

What you are seeing

  • The plot. The state x(t) in blue on the left axis, and its rate of change dx/dt in orange on the right axis, from t = 0 to tmax. Where a model's stock would go negative it is shown as zero.
  • Differential equation. The model being solved.
  • Analytical solution. Its closed-form solution, or a note on its equilibria. The two constant-harvest cases with H ≤ 1 are integrated numerically (fourth-order Runge-Kutta); the others are plotted from the closed form.
