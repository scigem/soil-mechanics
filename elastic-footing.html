Elastic Strip Footing

The displacement field in an elastic half-space under a uniformly loaded strip footing, from Boussinesq-type solutions: the Flamant line-load solution integrated across the strip, from −B to +B. The field assumes plane strain and linear elastic behaviour, so every displacement scales with q/E, and the Poisson ratio changes its shape.

What you are seeing

  • The contour plot. The chosen displacement component, in mm, over a section from −3B to 3B across the footing and down to the domain depth, drawn to equal scales. The vertical component uses a red–blue scale, the horizontal a red–yellow–blue scale, and the magnitude Viridis; the colour bar gives the values and the contours are labelled.
  • The footing. The black bar at the surface is the strip, and the red arrows are the uniform load on it.
