The Proctor Test: Why There is an Optimum

Set the moisture content and compact a sample. Dry of the optimum, water bridges squeeze the contacts and the blows cannot make them slide. Wet of it, the air is trapped and the water takes the blows. The optimum is where the air stops being able to get out. More energy beats more suction, so the optimum moves drier and denser along a line of constant saturation.
