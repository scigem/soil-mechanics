The Shear Box and Taylor's Balance

Push the top half of the box sideways under a fixed normal load. The work you do goes into sliding against friction and into lifting the load as the sample rises: τ/σ′ = tan φ′cs + dy/dx. A dense sample has to climb; a loose one sinks. Both end at the critical state.
